** AIO ARGB fans
The idea is to use the 3 fans of the AIO cooler mounted in the top of the chassis as system metric indicators.
|-----+--------+---------------------|
| Fan | Device | Sensor              |
|-----+--------+---------------------|
|   1 | CPU    | Package temperature |
|   2 | RAM    | Memory load in %    |
|   3 | GPU    | Core temperature    |
|-----+--------+---------------------|

The color of each ARGB is set according to the temperature (for CPU and GPU) and usage (for memory), following this table.
|---------+----------+--------------+----------|
| Hex     | CPU [°C] | RAM [% used] | GPU [°C] |
|---------+----------+--------------+----------|
| #0064FF |       40 |          N/A |       35 |
| #00FF00 |       55 |           25 |       50 |
| #FFC800 |       72 |           60 |       65 |
| #FF5000 |       85 |           80 |       77 |
| #FF0000 |       93 |           92 |       85 |
|---------+----------+--------------+----------|
*** CPU
AMD Ryzen 9 9950X.
- AMD’s maximum is 95 °C, with a 170 W TDP and a 230 W socket power limit.
- Typical readings are about 35–55 °C at idle, 55–75 °C while gaming, 70–90 °C in productivity work, and the low-to-mid 90s under sustained full load.
- One review measured a peak of 89 °C in Cinebench with a 360 mm AIO at full speed, and 60–72 °C in games.
|-------+---------+-------------------------------------------|
| Temp. | Colour  | Description                               |
|-------+---------+-------------------------------------------|
| 40 °C | #0064FF | Idle or cool                              |
| 55 °C | #00FF00 | Desktop use, light gaming                 |
| 72 °C | #FFC800 | Heavy gaming, compiling                   |
| 85 °C | #FF5000 | Sustained all-core load, hot but expected |
| 93 °C | #FF0000 | Right at the limit (throttling zone)      |
|-------+---------+-------------------------------------------|
*** RAM Memory
Corsair Vengeance 4 sticks of 32 GiB.
|------+---------+--------------|
| Load | Colour  | Memory usage |
|------+---------+--------------|
| 25 % | #00FF00 | 32 GiB       |
| 60 % | #FFC800 | 77 GiB       |
| 80 % | #FF5000 | 102 GiB      |
| 92 % | #FF0000 | 118 GiB      |
|------+---------+--------------|
*** GPU
ASUS PRIME OC GeForce RTX 5070 Ti.
- NVIDIA’s maximum for the RTX 5070 Ti is 88 °C, according to a guide that cites NVIDIA’s spec sheet.
- In reviews, the Prime OC peaked at about 66.8 °C and around 65 °C under heavy load.
- Its fans stay off below about 50 °C and don’t ramp up until temperatures pass roughly 55 °C, so light loads can sit in the 50s without it meaning anything.
|-------+---------+-----------------------------------------------|
| Temp. | Colour  | Description                                   |
|-------+---------+-----------------------------------------------|
| 35 °C | #0064FF | Idle or cool, fans in 0 dB mode               |
| 50 °C | #00FF00 | Desktop use, light gaming                     |
| 65 °C | #FFC800 | Heavy gaming, top of this card's normal range |
| 77 °C | #FF5000 | Hotter than usual, check airflow and dust     |
| 85 °C | #FF0000 | Right at the limit (88 °C max, throttling)    |
|-------+---------+-----------------------------------------------|
